Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Overview

ClipVault is a desktop application designed to help users identify gaming highlights, create short-form videos, and manage approved content for publishing.

2. Information We Process

ClipVault may process gaming footage, generated clips, video metadata, publishing preferences, and information provided by connected social media platforms.

3. Connected Accounts

When users authorize a supported platform, ClipVault may receive account identifiers and authorization tokens needed to access approved publishing features. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the user.

4. Local Storage

ClipVault is designed to process gaming footage locally. Application settings, clips, and publishing information may be stored on the user's device. Platform authorization credentials are intended to be stored securely.

5. Publishing

ClipVault is designed to publish content only after the user authorizes the connected platform and approves the content for posting.

6. Third-Party Platforms

When users publish content through TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, or other connected services, the applicable platform's own privacy policies and terms also apply.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored clips and related information can be removed from the user's device. Users may revoke connected-platform access through the platform's account settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as ClipVault's features and platform integrations develop.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to newclipperz40@gmail.com.
