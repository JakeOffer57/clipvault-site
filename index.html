ClipVault

ClipVault is a desktop application that helps creators identify gaming highlights, generate short-form videos, and manage their content in one place.

How ClipVault Works

ClipVault analyzes gaming footage to identify potential highlights. Users can review clips, select their favorites, and prepare approved videos for publishing.

Publishing Integrations

ClipVault is developing integrations with TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram to support approved video uploads and scheduled publishing, subject to platform permissions.

Privacy & User Control

Users control which clips are selected and approved. ClipVault is designed to process gaming footage locally.
